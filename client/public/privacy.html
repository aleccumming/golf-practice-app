Privacy Policy — Golf Practice

Last updated: September 2026

Golf Practice is a personal golf-tracking app. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Your name, email address, and profile picture, from Google Sign-In, used to identify your account.
  • Golf practice data you enter yourself — clubs, sessions, shots, putts, and practice plan progress — used to provide the app's tracking and pattern-detection features.

How we use it

Your data is used solely to operate the app for you: displaying your own history, detecting patterns in your own shots and putts, and generating practice plans. Your data is private to your account and is not visible to other users.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is stored in our database and not used for advertising.

Data deletion

You can delete individual shots, putts, sessions, or clubs directly in the app at any time. To delete your entire account and all associated data, contact us using the email below.
